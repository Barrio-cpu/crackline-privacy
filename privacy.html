Crackline

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Crackline collects no personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and makes no network connections. Your progress stays on your phone.

Who we are

Crackline is published by MUR Trading LLC. For any question about this policy, email rebecca@murtrading.com.

What the app stores

To keep your progress between sessions, Crackline saves the following on your device only:

  • Your game stats and coin total
  • The round you are currently playing
  • Your sound on/off setting

This information never leaves your device through the app. We cannot see it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it. If you use Android's own device backup, Android may include this data in your personal backup; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email or contact details are requested.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos are accessed.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • No in-app purchases. Coins are part of the game only and have no real-world value.

Rating the app on Google Play

After you have played a few rounds, Crackline may ask Google Play to show its standard rating window. That window is provided and run by Google Play, not by us. If you choose to leave a rating or review, it is sent to Google Play and published under Google's Privacy Policy. We never see it through the app and receive no other data from it.

Children

Crackline does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
